You are a player. You do not have source code, a repository, or admin tools.

1. Go to Account.

Then play:

1. Accept the current legal documents if asked.
2. Create a character in the Character Vault (quick-start is fine).
3. Create a campaign or accept the invite link you were given.
4. Seat your character and open the table.
5. Try Party Chat, Rules Desk, Address the DM, and Declare Action.
6. Try to act when it is not your turn. Write down what the site does.
7. Reload the page mid-session and confirm you still see your character.

whether you were blocked. Do not ask for APIs, emulators, or the codebase.
